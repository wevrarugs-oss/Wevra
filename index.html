Artisanship

Twelve Months on the Loom

In the highlands of Bamyan and the workshops of Herat, our weavers begin with hand-spun wool from Ghazni sheep — prized across Persia for its lustre, resilience and depth of colour. Every knot is tied by hand. Every dye is drawn from madder root, indigo, walnut husk and pomegranate rind. What emerges after a year at the loom is not a floor covering, but a document of a family, a village and a tradition that has survived centuries.
