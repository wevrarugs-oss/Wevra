Heritage & Craft

Authentic Afghan Handweaving

Each WEVRA rug represents months—and often up to a year—of laborious hand-knotting by skilled master artisans in northern Afghanistan. Utilizing hand-spun wool sheared from local Ghazni highland sheep, the threads are treated with centuries-old natural vegetable dye techniques derived from pomegranate skins, madder root, indigo, and walnut husks.

Unlike mass factory reproductions, an authentic hand-knotted rug retains organic warmth, resilient knot structure, and heirloom value that endures across generations.
