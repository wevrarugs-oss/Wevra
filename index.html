The hands behind every rug

Our rugs begin with the wool of mountain sheep, spun and dyed with madder root, walnut husk and indigo. A single piece can take a family many months to knot.

We buy from weavers in Bamyan, Kunduz and Herat at fair prices, and check every rug for knot density, symmetry and colour fastness before it leaves.

A rug is never finished. It simply passes to the next home.

Caring for your rug

Vacuum gently

Vacuum without the beater bar, following the direction of the pile. Never pull on the fringe.

Rotate yearly

Turn your rug every year so sunlight and foot traffic wear it evenly.

Act fast on spills

Blot, never rub, with a clean white cloth and cool water. Call a professional for stains.

Let it breathe

Use a breathable pad underneath. Store rolled with the pile inward, never folded.
