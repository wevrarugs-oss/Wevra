Generational Weaving Legacy

Preserving The Ancient Art Of Afghan Carpet Weaving

In the valleys of northern Afghanistan, rug weaving is not merely an industry—it is a living tradition passed down through centuries. Our weavers spin raw sheep wool by hand using drop spindles, creating organic variations in yarn thickness that give every WEVRA rug its distinct texture.

Dyes are derived entirely from organic sources: madder root for crimson, walnut husks for deep browns, indigo leaves for midnight blues, and pomegranate rind for warm golden tones. These natural dyes age gracefully, developing an irreplaceable vintage patina over decades.

Heirloom Care & Maintenance Guide

Hand-knotted wool carpets naturally repel dirt and last for generations when cared for with simple routines.

1. Vacuuming

Vacuum regularly using suction only. Avoid using beater bars on fringe edges to prevent fiber loosening.

2. Spill Response

Blot liquid spills immediately with a clean, un-dyed paper towel. Never rub liquids into the wool pile.

3. Rotation

Rotate your carpet 180 degrees once per year to balance foot traffic wear and natural sunlight exposure.
