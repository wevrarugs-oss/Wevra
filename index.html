Heirloom Care Guide

Handwoven rugs made from hand-spun Ghazni wool naturally repel dirt and last for generations when cared for properly.

Vacuuming

Vacuum regularly using suction only. Avoid rotating brush rollers on fringe edges.

Spill Response

Blot spills immediately with a clean dry cloth. Never rub liquid into wool fibers.

Rotation

Rotate your carpet 180 degrees once per year to ensure even wear and sun exposure.
