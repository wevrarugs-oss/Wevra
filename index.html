Preservation Guide

Caring For Hand-Knotted Rugs

With proper care, authentic Afghan rugs last for centuries and appreciate in vintage value.

Rotation & Direct Sunlight

Rotate your rug 180 degrees once every year to ensure equal wearing and balanced exposure to natural ambient light.

Vacuuming Technique

Vacuum regularly using suction only. Avoid powered beater bars that may pull delicate wool fringe threads.
