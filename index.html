Afghan Rug Care Guide

1. Vacuuming: Vacuum regularly using suction only. Avoid rotating brush attachments, which can snag natural hand-spun fibers.

2. Spill Care: Blot spills immediately with an un-dyed cloth. Never rub liquid into wool pile. Use lukewarm water with mild natural soap if needed.

3. Rotation: Rotate your carpet 180 degrees once a year to ensure uniform wear and even sun exposure.

4. Underlayment: Always use a quality rug pad under high-traffic carpets to prevent slipping and protect bottom knots.
